## What I do

<img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/M9gbBd9nbDrOTu1Mqx/giphy.gif" width="170" align="right" alt="" />

**Ship mobile apps that survive real users.** Five years native on Android and iOS, then Flutter and React Native since late 2024. Apps I have worked on serve millions of people a month, at 99%+ crash-free sessions.

**Make slow codebases fast again.** Unified two React Native apps that had drifted 2,300+ commits apart into one pnpm and Turborepo monorepo, upgraded RN 0.71 to 0.77, and cut an Android download from 60 MB to 36 MB.

**Own the path to production.** Automated iOS and Android releases on GitLab CI, Codemagic, and Fastlane, with Sentry and Datadog watching what happens after the build goes out.

**Raise the team, not just the code.** Led a 4 engineer iOS team, built design systems shipped as versioned packages across 4+ apps, and reviewed 540+ submissions at Dicoding at a 4.9 average rating.
